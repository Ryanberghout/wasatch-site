Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

Wasatch Lead Co ("we," "us") runs advertising for local home-service businesses. This policy explains how we handle information you submit through our ads and forms.

Information we collect

When you fill out one of our forms, including Facebook and Instagram lead forms, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, and details about the service you're asking about.

How we use it

We use your information to connect you with the local business you asked to hear from, so they can contact you about a quote, appointment or service. We may also use it to follow up on your request.

Sharing

We share your information only with the business you asked to hear from, and with service providers that help us run our forms and ads, such as Meta. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep lead information only as long as we need it to deliver the service, or as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing us. You can also opt out of calls or texts by telling the business that contacts you, or by contacting us.
